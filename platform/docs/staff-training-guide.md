# Staff Training Guide

Training should take less than five minutes.

## Core Message

GratiFi helps customers leave a direct thank-you after good service.

## Staff Script

“If you enjoyed the service, there’s a thank-you QR on the counter.”

## What Staff Should Avoid

- Do not pressure customers.
- Do not explain technical infrastructure.
- Do not promise rewards beyond the thank-you/tip flow.
- Do not ask customers to create accounts.

## Follow-Up Questions

- Did customers notice the QR?
- Did the staff script feel natural?
- Were there awkward moments?
- Should the QR move to a better location?
