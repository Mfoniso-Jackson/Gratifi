# Evidence Collection Guide

Evidence turns a pilot into a grant-ready story.

## Capture During Setup

- QR placement photo URL.
- Venue contact details.
- QR placement notes.
- Staff training completion.

## Capture During Follow-Up

- Owner feedback.
- Staff feedback.
- Customer feedback if shared.
- Testimonial text.
- Permission to use quote.
- Dashboard screenshot URL.
- Lessons learned and next steps.

## Case Study Readiness

A venue is case-study ready when:

- It has live recognition metrics.
- The QR checklist is mostly complete.
- Owner quote permission is recorded.
- Staff or owner feedback includes a clear operational lesson.
