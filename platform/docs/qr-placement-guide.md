# QR Placement Guide

QR placement should feel natural, visible, and easy to scan.

## Cafés

- Counter near payment terminal.
- Table tent near collection point.
- Small card by the tip jar if one exists.

## Barbershops

- Mirror station.
- Reception counter.
- Waiting area table.

## Rules of Thumb

- Keep the QR at eye or hand level.
- Avoid glossy surfaces that reflect light.
- Include one short staff prompt nearby.
- Test scan from a normal customer distance.
- Photograph the placement and add the URL to the venue pilot profile.
