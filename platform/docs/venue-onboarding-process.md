# Venue Onboarding Process

## Before the Visit

- Confirm owner or manager contact.
- Capture venue type and referral source.
- Prepare the venue QR and staff script.
- Bring printed QR cards and tape or display stands.

## During the Visit

1. Explain GratiFi as a thank-you and recognition tool.
2. Add workers who want to participate.
3. Show the owner the admin dashboard.
4. Place venue and worker QR codes.
5. Run one test recognition.
6. Schedule a follow-up date before leaving.

## After the Visit

- Mark the venue as `live`.
- Complete the checklist.
- Record QR placement notes.
- Add photos or dashboard screenshots as evidence links.
