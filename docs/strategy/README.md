# GratiFi strategy

**North star:** GratiFi becomes the global platform where frontline workers get paid, recognised
and hired. Tipping in UK venues is the wedge, not the destination.

The full strategy documents (Path to Unicorn, Pilot Launch Plan, AI Playbook, and Blockchain and
Web3 Playbook) are kept privately by the owner; this page is the summary the code follows.

**What customers want:** [customer-assumptions.md](customer-assumptions.md) lists what tippers, staff
and owners want, the evidence, and how the pilot tests each assumption. The riskiest: owners paying
for recognition.

**The competition:** [competition.md](competition.md). QR and NFC tipping is already crowded and
funded (Strikepay, TiPJAR, EasyTip, card machines). GratiFi competes on recognition and keeping
staff, not on the tip itself.

## The stages

| Stage | Years | What it proves | Gate to the next stage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Prove the UK | 1 | 3-venue pilot, then 50 paying venues | Owners pay; staff read their notes weekly |
| 2. UK and Ireland | 1 to 2 | 500 venues; first till or rota software partner | Sales repeat without the founder |
| 3. Enter the US | 2 to 4 | A US lighthouse city; worker accounts | Strong, repeatable US growth |
| 4. Global platform | 4 to 7 | Canada, Australia, Europe via partners; hiring and worker money tools | Several revenue streams at global scale |

**We are in stage 1.** Feature freeze until the pilot has 30 days of data (decision on 31 December
2026). Code changes are fixes for what the test run and pilot venues turn up.

## Where the revenue comes from

Five streams: venue subscriptions, a cut of tips and payouts, worker money tools, hiring, and brands
and sponsors. Subscriptions alone can't reach a global-scale business, which is why the worker account
and tip volume across countries matter.

## The flywheel

Venue adds GratiFi, then customers leave tips and notes, then workers build a record and get paid,
then workers change jobs and bring their account, then new venues join to hire and keep recognised
staff. The worker account that moves between jobs and countries is the network effect.

## Principles

1. **Prove before expanding.** No new country or big feature until the current stage's gate is met.
2. **Workers are the long-term user.** Venues pay now; workers carry GratiFi to their next job.
3. **Compliance is a feature.** Tipping laws (UK 2023, Ireland 2022, Ontario 2016, US federal rules)
   are a reason to use GratiFi; build to them in each country.
4. **GratiFi never holds tip money** until it has a licence or a licensed partner. Stripe Connect pays
   venues directly.
5. **The blockchain stays invisible.** It's proof, not a product: no tokens, wallets or crypto words
   for customers and staff.
6. **Build global-ready, launch local.** New code shouldn't add UK-only assumptions where avoiding them
   is cheap. Existing ones are listed in [globalisation-debt.md](globalisation-debt.md).

## What to measure

Stage 1: scans that become tips (over 30%), tips with a note (over 50%), staff reading their tips
weekly (over 60%), owners who'd pay after 30 days (2 of 3). From stage 2: tip volume through
GratiFi, revenue growth, venues joining by referral, and worker accounts that move between venues.
